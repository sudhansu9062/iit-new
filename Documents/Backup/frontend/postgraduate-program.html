Academics / Postgraduate

Master of Science in Physics

An advanced two-year program focused on deep theoretical training, high-precision experimentation and interdisciplinary research preparation.

Program Overview

Specialized learning for emerging physicists

The postgraduate curriculum is designed for students who want to transition into research and advanced industry roles. The program combines rigorous coursework, modern instrumentation training and guided independent inquiry.

2Year Duration
64+Credit Hours
18+Advanced Electives
1:6Faculty Ratio

Curriculum

Two years of advanced specialization

Semester 1

Core Concepts

  • Advanced Quantum Mechanics
  • Classical Electrodynamics
  • Mathematical Physics
  • Computational Modeling

Semester 2

Methods

  • Statistical Field Theory
  • Experimental Methods
  • Spectroscopy
  • Numerical Analysis

Semester 3

Electives

  • Nanophotonics
  • Quantum Information
  • Condensed Matter Systems
  • Astrophysical Methods

Semester 4

Thesis

  • Research Project
  • Seminar Defense
  • Technical Communication
  • Publication Orientation

Opportunities

Pathways to research and innovation

Lab Apprenticeship

Hands-on work with advanced instruments in optics, materials and computational physics labs.

Industry Projects

Collaborative projects with R&D partners in semiconductors, analytics and scientific computing.

Conference Support

Department-backed participation in national and international conferences.

PhD Preparation

Structured mentorship for doctoral admissions, proposals and research statement development.
