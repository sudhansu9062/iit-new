HPC Cluster Access

Two High-Performance Computing (HPC) Cluster, SNBOSE, PRITHVI managed by the Department of Physics, IIT Kharagpur. Access to the cluster is granted exclusively through secure shell (SSH) to the designated cluster, which serves as the gateway to the compute resources. All computational work must be submitted via the SLURM job scheduler, direct execution of resource-intensive tasks on the login node is strictly prohibited.

Please complete this form to request access credentials.

Note: Upon form submission, an automated notification is dispatched to the HPC Admin. Once processed, your login details will be shared via your registered email.
