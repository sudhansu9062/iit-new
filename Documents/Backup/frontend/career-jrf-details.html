Junior Research Fellow (JRF)

Department of Physics · Quantum Materials Research

Position

Junior Research Fellow (JRF)

Quantum Materials Research

Location

IIT Kharagpur, West Bengal, India

Job Type

Fellowship (Full-time)

Application Deadline

15 March 2026

Job Description

Applications are invited for a Junior Research Fellow (JRF) position under a SERB-funded project on synthesis and characterisation of novel quantum materials. The selected candidate will work on cutting-edge research involving the growth of single crystals, thin films, and nanostructures of quantum materials, and will perform advanced characterisation using techniques such as X-ray diffraction, magnetometry, and transport measurements. This is an excellent opportunity for motivated researchers to contribute to frontier areas of condensed matter physics.

Key Responsibilities

  • Synthesis of novel quantum materials including single crystals and thin films
  • Characterisation using XRD, magnetometry, Raman spectroscopy, and transport measurements
  • Analysis and interpretation of experimental data
  • Literature survey and staying updated with recent developments in the field
  • Assisting in the preparation of research publications and project reports
  • Maintenance of laboratory equipment and inventory

Eligibility Criteria

  • M.Sc. in Physics with a minimum of 60% marks or equivalent CGPA
  • Valid GATE or NET (JRF) qualification is mandatory
  • Experience in experimental condensed matter physics is preferred
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Good written and verbal communication skills in English

How to Apply

Steps

  1. Prepare your application with all required documents
  2. Send the application via email to the PI with the subject line: 'Application for JRF - Quantum Materials'
  3. Shortlisted candidates will be contacted for an interview

Required Documents

  • Updated Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  • Scanned copies of academic transcripts and certificates
  • GATE/NET scorecard
  • Statement of Research Interest (1 page)
  • Contact details of two academic referees

Application Method

Email application to: pi.quantum@phy.iitkgp.ac.in
