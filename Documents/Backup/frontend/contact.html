How to Reach IIT Kharagpur

The Department of Physics is situated inside the main academic complex of the IIT Kharagpur campus.

🚆 By Rail (Train)

Kharagpur Junction (KGP) is a major railway hub (~5 km from campus) connected by direct express trains to Howrah/Kolkata, Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, and Bengaluru. Taxis, auto-rickshaws, and e-rickshaws (totos) are available 24/7 at the station to reach IIT Kharagpur campus (15–20 mins).

🚗 By Car / Road

IIT Kharagpur is connected to Kolkata via the 4-lane NH-16 (old NH-6, Mumbai-Kolkata Highway). Driving distance from Kolkata via Vidyasagar Setu (Kona Expressway) is approx. 120 km (2 to 2.5 hours drive). Enter Kharagpur via Chowringhee and proceed directly to the campus main gate.

✈️ By Flight (Air)

The nearest airport is Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose International Airport (CCU) in Kolkata (~140 km). From the airport, you can hire a pre-paid taxi/cab directly to campus (approx. 2.5–3 hours), or take airport transit to Howrah Railway Station and board an express train to Kharagpur (~1.5 hours).
