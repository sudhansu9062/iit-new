Condensed Matter Physics

OVERVIEW

Condensed Matter Physics focuses on understanding the physical properties of solids and liquids, including electronic, magnetic, and structural behavior. Research in this area spans from fundamental quantum phenomena to applied materials science.

RESEARCH TOPICS

  • Quantum Materials
  • Nanostructures
  • Soft Matter Physics
  • Electronic Transport
  • Magnetism & Spintronics
  • Superconductivity
