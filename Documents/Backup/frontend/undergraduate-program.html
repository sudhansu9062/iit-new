Academics / Undergraduate

Bachelor of Science in Physics

A four-year, research-driven undergraduate program building rigorous foundations in theoretical, experimental and computational physics.

Program Overview

An immersive foundation in modern physics

Our undergraduate curriculum balances classical pillars with frontiers of contemporary research. Students engage with quantum information, condensed matter, photonics, astrophysics and computational methods while developing strong mathematical and laboratory skills.

4Year Duration
120+Credit Hours
30+Elective Courses
1:8Faculty Ratio

Curriculum

Four years, structured for depth

Year 1

Foundations

  • Classical Mechanics
  • Calculus & Linear Algebra
  • Modern Physics
  • Lab Techniques I

Year 2

Core Physics

  • Electromagnetism
  • Thermodynamics
  • Mathematical Methods
  • Lab Techniques II

Year 3

Advanced Topics

  • Quantum Mechanics
  • Statistical Physics
  • Solid State Physics
  • Computational Physics

Year 4

Specialization

  • Electives
  • Senior Project
  • Research Seminar
  • Professional Development

Opportunities

Beyond the classroom

Research Internships

Work alongside faculty in active research labs from your second year onward.

Global Exchange

Semester programs at partner institutions across Europe, Asia and North America.

Innovation Projects

Funded student-led projects in instrumentation, computation and applied physics.

Mentorship

One-to-one faculty mentoring from year one through final-year capstone.
