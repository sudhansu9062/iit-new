Research

The Department of Physics at IIT Kharagpur conducts research across a wide spectrum of theoretical, experimental, and interdisciplinary domains. The following areas represent the major research directions within the department.

Condensed Matter Physics

Study of physical properties of matter in solid and soft phases.

High Energy Physics

Astrophysics & Cosmology
