About the Conference

International Conference on Condensed Matter Physics (ICCMP 2026) brings together researchers, faculty, and students to discuss current advances in materials science, quantum matter, computational methods, and instrumentation.

Sessions will include invited talks, technical paper presentations, workshops, and panel discussions hosted at IIT Kharagpur.

Dates

10-13 May 2026

Venue

Netaji Auditorium, IIT Kharagpur

Participants

300+ delegates from 35 institutions

Organized By

Department of Physics, IIT Kharagpur
