Privacy Policy

The Department of Physics, Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, is committed to protecting the privacy of all visitors to this website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data. By using this website, you agree to the terms outlined below.

Information We Collect

  • We collect personal information such as name, email address, phone number, and institutional affiliation only when voluntarily submitted through our Contact Us form or Facility Booking form.
  • Alumni registration may require additional details such as graduation year, programme, and current professional information, provided voluntarily by the user.
  • We do not collect sensitive personal data such as financial information, biometric data, or government-issued identification numbers through this website.
  • Server logs may automatically record your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and pages visited for the purpose of website administration and security monitoring.

How We Use Your Information

  • Personal information submitted through the Contact Us form is used solely to respond to your queries and provide the requested information about the Department.
  • Facility Booking form submissions are used to process and manage booking requests for departmental laboratories, equipment, and other shared resources.
  • Alumni data is used to maintain a verified alumni network, facilitate departmental communications, and support the Giving & Support programme.
  • Server log data is used exclusively for website performance monitoring, security analysis, and improving user experience. It is not used to personally identify individual visitors.
  • We do not use any collected information for marketing, advertising, or commercial purposes.

Data Sharing & Disclosure

  • We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share your personal information with any third parties for commercial purposes.
  • Personal data may be shared internally within the Department of Physics and with relevant administrative offices of IIT Kharagpur only as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected.
  • Information may be disclosed if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request in accordance with applicable Indian laws.
  • Aggregated, anonymised statistical data about website usage may be used for institutional reporting and does not identify any individual user.

Data Security

  • We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
  • All form submissions are transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to safeguard data in transit.
  • Access to personal data is restricted to authorised departmental personnel who require it for legitimate administrative purposes.
  • While we take reasonable precautions to protect your information, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

External Links

  • This website contains links to external websites, including the IIT Kharagpur main site, ERP portal, and Webmail service. These external sites are governed by their own privacy policies.
  • The Department of Physics is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of any linked external websites.
  • We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external sites before providing personal information.

Your Rights

  • You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • You may request correction or deletion of your personal data by contacting the Department office.
  • You may choose not to provide personal information through our forms; however, this may limit your ability to use certain features such as contacting the Department or booking facilities.
  • Alumni may request removal from the alumni directory at any time by contacting the departmental office.

Changes to This Policy

  • The Department of Physics reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time without prior notice.
  • Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
  • We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

Questions or Concerns?

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your personal data is handled, please contact us at phyoffice@phy.iitkgp.ac.in or call +91-3222-281285 (Office).
