Research / Labs & Facilities

Where physics happens

Our laboratories and shared facilities span the spectrum from quantum materials cleanrooms to astrophysics observatories, built for collaborative frontier research.

Laboratories

Six flagship research labs

Quantum Materials & Low-Temperature Physics

Condensed Matter Lab

Dilution refrigerators, transport measurements and angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy for the study of correlated electron systems.

Ultrafast Lasers & Quantum Optics

Optics & Photonics Lab

Femtosecond pump-probe systems, single-photon sources and integrated photonics testbeds for precision optical experiments.

Computational Frontier

High Energy Physics Lab

Dedicated GPU cluster for lattice QCD, event simulation and machine-learning analysis of collider and detector data.

Class-100 Fabrication

Nanoscience Cleanroom

Electron-beam lithography, thin-film deposition and SEM/AFM imaging for nanoscale device fabrication and prototyping.

Optical & Radio Observation

Astrophysics Observatory

On-campus telescope facility paired with remote access to international observatories for time-domain and cosmology studies.

Imaging & Single Molecule

Biophysics Lab

Confocal and super-resolution microscopy alongside optical-tweezer setups for soft-matter and biological physics research.
