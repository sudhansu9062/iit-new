About the Department

Department of Physics, Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur

History

The Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur was established in 1951 as the first of the IITs, marking the beginning of a new era in technical education in India. The institute was conceived to serve as a center of excellence in science, engineering, and technology, contributing to national development.

Over the decades, IIT Kharagpur has expanded into a multidisciplinary institution with a strong emphasis on teaching, research, and innovation. The institute has developed extensive academic infrastructure and fostered collaborations with leading institutions worldwide.

The Department of Physics has been a core academic unit of IIT Kharagpur since its early years. The department has played a vital role in providing foundational physics education and advancing research across a broad spectrum of theoretical and experimental areas.

Today, the department continues to contribute significantly to academic excellence, research output, and interdisciplinary initiatives, while adapting to emerging areas of science and technology.
