Why Your Support Matters

The Department of Physics at IIT Kharagpur has a long-standing tradition of excellence in research and education. Contributions from alumni, industry partners, and well-wishers enable us to:

  • Support student scholarships and fellowships
  • Upgrade laboratory infrastructure
  • Fund advanced research projects
  • Invite distinguished speakers and scholars
  • Support conference travel for students

Areas You Can Support

🎓

Student Scholarships

Provide financial assistance to meritorious and deserving students pursuing undergraduate and postgraduate studies in physics.

🔬

Research Infrastructure

Support acquisition of advanced experimental and computational facilities that enable cutting-edge research.

🏆

Endowed Chairs & Fellowships

Establish named chairs or fellowships in specific research domains to attract and retain exceptional faculty.

📅

Events & Academic Initiatives

Support workshops, conferences, and outreach programs that enrich the academic community.
